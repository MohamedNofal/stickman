# Stickman privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Stickman (Amazon Appstore edition, package `com.clean.stickman.tv`) is made by Mohamed Nofal.

**Stickman collects no personal data.** It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it never sends anything about you, your TV or your apps to the developer or to anyone else.

## What stays on your Fire TV

- Your button mappings and settings are stored on the device only.
- To remap the remote and manage apps, Stickman starts a local VPN connection. It carries only Stickman's own commands to the Fire TV's built-in debugging service on the same device. No other traffic goes through it, and nothing leaves the device through it.
- Stickman reads the list of installed and running apps and how much memory they use, only to show them to you on screen.

## Network use

Stickman makes one kind of internet request: the optional **Fix clock** button in Settings reads the current date from www.google.com to correct a wrong system clock. Nothing about you is sent with it.

## Children

Stickman is a utility for adults setting up their TV and isn't directed at children.
